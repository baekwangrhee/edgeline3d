What we do

We build the whole object — surface to signal.

Most projects mean juggling a printer, a chip supplier, and a web developer. We do all three under one roof, so the part, the tag, and the page it opens are designed to work together.

01 / DESIGN

Design & prototyping

From a sketch or an idea to a working CAD model, engineered around the chip, the coil, and the magnet from day one.

02 / MANUFACTURE

3D printing & small batch

In-house additive manufacturing in PETG, PLA, and TPU. From a single prototype to production runs of hundreds.

03 / CONNECT

NFC integration & web

Embedded NTAG chips, encoding, and the web layer each tap opens — the page, link, or record that makes the object smart.

Process

Four steps, one team.

The same people carry a project from the first CAD sketch to a tag that opens a live page.

STEP 01

Design

Model the housing around the NFC coil, ferrite shield, and magnets so the part performs and prints cleanly.

STEP 02

Print

Additive manufacturing, layer by layer, in durable materials chosen for the object's job and finish.

STEP 03

Embed

Seat the chip and shielding, snap the housing shut. No visible seams, no glue, no re-opening.

STEP 04

Encode & deploy

Program each tag to its URL and stand up the page it opens — editable long after the object ships.
